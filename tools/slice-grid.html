Card grid slicer

Cuts a ChatGPT grid image into individual card PNGs, named to match the card ids the app expects. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Drop the image here, or click to choose a file
Heads up: your browser may ask to allow multiple downloads — say yes. Files land in your downloads folder; move them into public/assets/cards/. Cells marked discard are filler and won't be saved.
